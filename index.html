A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Art is how I tell stories.

I'm a 2D artist focused on character design, illustration, and visual storytelling. I enjoy turning ideas into expressive artwork through thoughtful composition, color, and detail. My goal is to create visuals that feel memorable, distinctive, and full of personality.

Whether shaping dynamic silhouettes for game heroes, drafting background worldbuilding for animated scenes, or experimenting with digital painting palettes, I treat every canvas as an opportunity to spark emotion and wonder.
